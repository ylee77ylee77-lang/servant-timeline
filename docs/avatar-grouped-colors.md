# Avatar feature-grouped colors

- Seven main cards for existing masculine/feminine portraits: role, face, hairstyle, eyewear, outfit, background and animal. Existing first-time role selection is unchanged. Main sections are mutually exclusive; one nested color may open within the selected main section. Switching a feature closes its nested color controls.
- Existing available skin, hair, frame, lens and outfit color channels are grouped under their corresponding features. No unsupported channels are added. No glasses hides frame/lens controls; removing the glasses does not erase their preferences. Lens transparency remains inside its lens color card. Outfit color channels follow the actual garment, and existing compatible-color retention is unchanged.
- No changes to artwork, stored IDs, normalizer, profile APIs, database, or registration/profile preview. All color editing still uses the same shared wheel. Draft wheel coordinates are held by the editor across section changes, including zero brightness/saturation.
- Unit tests cover ownership, nested disclosure and compatible colors; real server-rendered editor markup verifies seven main cards, text-only collapsed state, open nested controls, and conditional eyewear/outfit channels. Existing renderer and application/database verification remain required. Preview and iPhone acceptance remain separate from automated proof.
